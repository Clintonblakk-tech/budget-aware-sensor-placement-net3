Detection-time results are conditional on successful detection. Events that were not detected do not contribute a detection time. This outcome must therefore be interpreted alongside detection rate.

These tests assess differences among the three Net3 junction-only budget groups. They do not establish generalisability to other water distribution networks.

The tests use asymptotic p-values. With ten independent training runs per group, inferential findings should be interpreted cautiously.

This report is a derived statistical analysis of the existing experiment. It does not rerun DQN training or modify original experimental records.
